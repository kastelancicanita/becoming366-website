1. Digital Products

Becoming 366 currently sells digital products, including downloadable planners, printable materials and other digital resources.

These are digital products and no physical product will be shipped unless a particular product listing expressly states otherwise.

Please review the product description, included files, formats and compatibility information carefully before purchasing.

2. Purchases Through Etsy

Becoming 366 products are currently purchased and delivered through Etsy.

When you purchase through Etsy, your transaction and digital delivery are processed through Etsy and are also subject to Etsy’s applicable terms, policies and checkout process.

Becoming366.com does not currently process payments or deliver purchased files directly.

3. Returns and Exchanges

Because our current products are digital downloads, they cannot be physically returned or exchanged in the same way as physical goods.

Etsy does not provide standard return or exchange settings for digital listings.

Where applicable, the Etsy checkout process may ask buyers purchasing digital content to consent to immediate access or delivery and acknowledge the effect this has on their statutory right of withdrawal.

Any statutory rights that cannot legally be excluded remain unaffected by this Refund Policy.

4. Change of Mind

Subject to applicable consumer law and any rights that cannot legally be waived, refunds are generally not provided simply because a buyer:

  • changes their mind after obtaining access to or downloading the digital product;
  • decides they no longer want or need the product;
  • purchases the product by mistake;
  • does not use the product;
  • expected a physical product where the listing clearly identified the product as a digital download; or
  • lacks compatible software or equipment where the relevant requirements were clearly stated before purchase.

Nothing in this section removes any mandatory consumer right that applies in the buyer’s circumstances.

5. Problems With Your Digital Product

We want you to receive the product that was described and purchased.

Please contact us if:

  • the file is corrupted or cannot be opened despite using appropriate compatible software;
  • a file that should have been included is missing;
  • you received the wrong file;
  • the digital product is materially different from its description; or
  • another genuine technical problem prevents you from accessing or using the purchased files as described.

Where reasonably possible, we may first attempt to resolve the issue by providing technical assistance, a corrected file or a replacement copy.

Where applicable law gives you a right to another remedy, including a refund, those rights remain unaffected.

6. Download and Device Issues

Before purchasing, please check the product description for information about file formats, printing, software or device compatibility.

Becoming 366 cannot guarantee compatibility with software, applications, printers or devices that are not stated as supported in the relevant product description.

Please note that Etsy’s method of accessing digital downloads may differ depending on the device or platform being used.

If you experience difficulty locating or downloading a purchase through Etsy, please first review the download information available through your Etsy account or contact us for assistance.

7. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the exact same digital product more than once, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will review duplicate-purchase requests individually and, where appropriate and technically possible, may refund the duplicate transaction.

8. Unauthorised Sharing or Misuse

Refunds will not be provided as a means of obtaining digital files for free after downloading, copying, distributing or otherwise using them in violation of the applicable licence or our Terms of Use.

Unauthorised sharing, resale or redistribution of Becoming 366 digital products is prohibited.

This section does not affect any statutory rights that cannot legally be excluded.

9. How to Request Help

For purchases made through Etsy, you may contact us through Etsy’s order-help or messaging system.

You may also contact:

hello@becoming366.com

Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant purchase and understand the issue.

Do not send payment-card information by email.

10. Refund Processing

Where a refund is required or approved for an Etsy purchase, it will normally be processed through Etsy using the payment and refund mechanisms available for the original transaction.

Processing times after a refund is issued may depend on Etsy, the payment provider and the buyer’s financial institution.

11. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to exclude, restrict or replace rights or remedies that a consumer has under mandatory applicable consumer-protection law.

For consumers in the European Union or European Economic Area, specific statutory rules apply to digital content and the right of withdrawal.

Where a consumer has expressly consented to the immediate supply of digital content and acknowledged the loss of the statutory right of withdrawal in accordance with applicable law, the right of withdrawal may cease once performance begins as provided by law.

Other statutory rights, including rights relating to digital content that is defective or does not conform to the contract, remain unaffected.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy if our products, sales platforms, business practices or applicable legal requirements change.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

A policy change will not remove rights that a buyer already has under applicable law in relation to an earlier purchase.
