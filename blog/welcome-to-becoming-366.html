Why I started writing here

Not a launch announcement. Just — I needed a place that wasn't Instagram and wasn't Etsy.

I've been sitting on this for a while, not sure if I should even publish it. But that's kind of the point of having a blog, isn't it.

Instagram is fine for pretty things. Etsy is fine for selling things. Neither is great for the middle — the Tuesday when you stare at the ceiling for twenty minutes before you get up, or the chapter in a book that made you cry on the bus, or the week you didn't touch your planner at all and felt guilty about it.

I wanted a place for that.

So this is me writing — not as a brand, not as a shop owner, just as someone who's trying to figure things out and made a planner along the way because I needed something that didn't exist yet.

Some posts will be short. Some will be me processing something out loud. Some will be about a book I read and couldn't stop thinking about. I don't have a posting schedule and I'm not going to pretend I do.

If that sounds like something you'd read — you're welcome here. If you just want the planner, that's on Etsy and that's fine too.

More soon. Or not soon. We'll see.
